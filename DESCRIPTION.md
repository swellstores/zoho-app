# Zoho Books & Inventory for Swell

Keep your Swell store and Zoho in step, automatically. Products, customers, orders, payments, stock and shipments move between Swell and Zoho on their own, so your books and your inventory are always up to date and nobody has to copy anything by hand.

Works with **Zoho Books**, **Zoho Inventory**, or both, in every Zoho region: zoho.com, zoho.eu, zoho.in, zoho.com.au, zoho.jp, zohocloud.ca, zoho.sa and zoho.uk.

## Your catalog in Zoho

Every product and variant in your Swell store gets a matching item in Zoho.

- Items you already have in Zoho are found by SKU and linked, without changing them.
- Missing items are created for you, each variant as its own item, with your current stock as the opening stock.
- Change a name, SKU or price in Swell, and the Zoho item follows.
- One **Sync products** button brings your whole existing catalog over, with live progress and a clear list of anything Zoho could not accept.

## Every order in your books

- **Placed orders** appear in Zoho as sales orders, which reserve the stock.
- **Paid orders** get an invoice and the payment, ready for your accountant.
- **Customers** become Zoho contacts with their first order, addresses included.
- **Discounts, shipping and taxes** come along on every line. Each tax is matched to the Zoho tax with the same rate.
- **Cancellations and refunds** are reversed in Zoho. An order canceled before it ships is undone as if it never happened. A shipped order that is refunded gets a credit note and the refund.

Every order in your Swell dashboard shows its sales order, invoice and credit note in Zoho, with direct links.

## Stock you manage in one place

Zoho Inventory becomes the one place to manage stock. Receive goods, make adjustments, move stock between locations and handle returns in Zoho, and Swell follows within seconds.

Sold units that Zoho has not recorded yet are held back, so Swell never offers stock that is already on its way to a customer. If stock is changed directly in Swell, for example when a return is put back on the shelf, Swell is set back to Zoho's number, so the two never drift apart.

## Shipments with tracking

Ship an order in Zoho, and the Swell order is marked as shipped, with the carrier and tracking number. If shipping emails are on in Swell, your customer gets one. Partial shipments and tracking numbers added later work too.

## Set up in minutes

A guided page in your Swell dashboard takes you through the setup:

1. Create a Zoho API client. The page links to the right Zoho console for your region and gives you the address to paste.
2. Paste the client details into the app settings.
3. Press **Connect to Zoho** and approve access.
4. Press **Sync products**.
5. Press **Set up in Zoho**, and the app creates the Zoho rules that send stock changes and shipments to Swell.

## Always know what's happening

The Zoho page shows at a glance:

- the Zoho organization you're connected to;
- catalog sync progress, and any product Zoho couldn't accept, with the reason;
- orders waiting to be sent again, with a **Retry now** button;
- whether each Zoho rule is set up, and when Zoho last sent an update.

Orders that can't be sent right away, for example because a tax rate is missing in Zoho, are retried automatically, and the message tells you exactly what to fix.

## Good to know

- Zoho Books alone gives you contacts, items, invoices and payments. Stock and shipments need Zoho Inventory.
- Amounts are sent to Zoho as they are, without currency conversion, so use the same currency in Swell and Zoho. The app warns you if they differ.
- Not synced yet: partial refunds, bundles, gift cards, Swell stock locations, and order lines with more than one tax.
